## Key Findings

### Honest-Fit Thesis
- Thesis is correct ("local small model is the only honest fit for a private diary")
- But needs an **emotional hook**, not an intellectual one — lead with a concrete privacy scare story ("a friend's journal got uploaded to cloud AI training"), not abstract "diaries are private"

### The "Wow" Moment — Memory Callback
- Memory callback IS the wow, but it's fragile. **Seed the database with synthetic entries** to guarantee it fires in the demo/video
- Consider adding a **live similarity score badge** ("🔗 0.71 similarity to entry from 3 days ago") to make the recall feel intelligent, not magical
- **Design tension:** Unbidden memory recall may feel violating if the entry was emotionally dark. Show the user being in control.
